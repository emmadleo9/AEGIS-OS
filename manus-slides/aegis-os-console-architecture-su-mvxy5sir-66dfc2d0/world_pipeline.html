WorldView Data Pipeline

INGESTION
→
OSINT FEED POLLING
Asynchronous polling of public ADS-B (api.adsb.lol) and USGS earthquake GeoJSON feeds with custom retry logic and connection failover.
TRANSFORM
→
GEOJSON NORMALIZATION
Normalizing raw telemetry into standard FeatureCollections; implementing aircraft capping (max 300) and magnitude filtering.
RENDER
→
MAPLIBRE GL GLOBE
Hardware-accelerated 3D globe projection utilizing Carto Dark Matter styles and dynamic symbol rotation based on aircraft track vectors.
